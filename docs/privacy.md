# Privacy Policy

**Moyo** collects nothing.

The application has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no crash
reporting. It never contacts a server — it is built without the network
entitlement, so macOS itself would refuse the attempt.

## What stays on your Mac

The Go engine and its neural networks are bundled inside the application. Every
game you play is computed locally and written only inside the application's own
sandbox container, on your machine. Nothing is transmitted, and there is nothing
for us to see, store, or share.

## Third parties

There are none. No advertising, no software development kit, no service
provider, no data processor.

## Children

The application is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
children included.

## Changes

If a future version ever collected anything, this page would say so before that
version shipped, and the App Store privacy details would be updated to match.
